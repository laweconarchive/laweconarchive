# Research Weekly Issue 005 — Search and quality record

## Scope

- Search date: 24 August 2026 (China Standard Time).
- Publication window: 18–24 August 2026.
- Included output types: newly posted or materially revised peer-reviewed papers, accepted papers, working papers, and discussion papers with direct law-and-economics, institutional, organizational, regulatory, public-policy, or household-finance relevance.
- Excluded: news reports, events, commentary without an underlying paper, records without enough primary-source metadata to summarize methods and findings, and papers whose official source did not establish that the item fell within the weekly window.

## Sources and screening

The scan checked current official publication records from CEPR and discovery results from NBER and ECGI. The CEPR recent-publications list contained twelve discussion papers dated 21–23 August 2026. Six were retained after title-and-abstract screening for direct relevance to the archive; six were excluded as outside the editorial scope of this issue. NBER candidates whose official pages gave only an August 2026 issue month were not used to assert an exact within-week release date.

All six included records were checked on the official CEPR paper page for title, authors, publication date, paper number, abstract, method or data description, principal result, and stable source URL. The public summaries distinguish theoretical, empirical, experimental, model-based, and review claims and include an interpretation caution for every paper.

## Quality assessment

- Source integrity: pass. Every included record has an official repository page and exact publication date.
- Claim discipline: pass. No conclusion is stated more strongly than the official abstract supports.
- Output status: all six are CEPR Discussion Papers; none is represented as peer reviewed or finally published.
- Venue distribution: advisory. CEPR accounts for 6/6 included papers. This concentration reflects the narrow exact-date window and availability of sufficient relevant CEPR releases, but it limits representativeness. The public coverage note discloses the concentration.
- Geographic and methodological breadth: mixed but acceptable for a weekly feed. The issue covers U.S., Finnish, South African, Spanish, English, and cross-market settings and includes theory, matched panels, quasi-experimental designs, a randomized survey experiment, life-cycle modeling, and a review.
- Expanded lookback: not used.
